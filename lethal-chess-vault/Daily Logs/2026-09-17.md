# 2026-09-17 · The phone gets its own shape

Daily log. See [[Home]], [[System Map]], [[Decision Log]], [[Visual Design]].

## Summary

The owner tested [[Exploration Mode]] on a phone and found the screen too tall for the window. Three
instructions followed, and each is now a rule rather than a tweak:

1. **The site bar is one row on a phone**, glyphs instead of words (82px → 52px).
2. **The opening's name and its first moves are written once per screen.** Explore had them three times:
   the panel header, the idle line card, and the notice.
3. **Mode switching left the session screen.** Explore / Practice / The Open is the [[Opening dashboard]]'s
   job; the phone gets *← Dashboard* and *Moves & progress* instead.

Underneath those, the real change: **every board screen is now an app shell that cannot scroll.** See
[[Decision Log]], 2026-09-17.

## Measured

A CDP sweep of every view at 390 × {844, 740, 667}, before and after. Page overflow in pixels:

| View | 844 before | 844 after | 740 after | 667 after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/today` | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| `/openings/[id]` | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| `/openings/[id]/explore` | 15 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| `/openings/[id]/practice` | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| `/openings/[id]/open` | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| `/play` | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

The "before" zeros are misleading: at 844 the content ended within a pixel or two of the fold and the
cut-off blocks were the mode row and the sheet button. At 740 and 667 the same views spilled by 14–125px.
`/` and `/settings` are lists and still scroll, by design.

## Found while measuring

**The openings list was zooming the whole page out.** `.spine i` is 3px wide with a 2px gap and the
Queen's Gambit has 94 variations — a 468px row inside a 390px window, so Chrome widened the layout
viewport to 501px and shrank *everything* on the page to fit. Capped at 40 segments, the tail folded into
one. Now `src/lib/ui/spine.ts`, with `spine.test.ts` reading the two pixel values out of the stylesheet
so the cap and the widths cannot drift apart.

## Left alone

- The Open's round card names the opening, and so does the header under it. Different subjects (the round
  vs. the screen), and stripping the name leaves a card that is a kicker and eight pips.
- `/settings` scrolls. It is a form; the app-shell rule is for screens with a board on them.
- `/play` at 667 scrolls its controls column by 7px — the designed fallback, not the window.
