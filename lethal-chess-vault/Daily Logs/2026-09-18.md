# 2026-09-18 · Settings stops being a place

Daily log. See [[Home]], [[System Map]], [[Decision Log]], [[Settings as a layer]].

## Summary

The owner: changing the theme mid-practice meant going to Settings and coming back to a reset screen.
Settings is now a **layer** — opened over the page by SvelteKit shallow routing under a native modal
dialog, closed by `history.back()` — and the screen beneath keeps its session. The site bar reads the
difference: places are words on the left, the gear is a glyph on the right with the account.

The design work was the category, not the one page: which screens are *places* and which are *layers*,
by three tests. Report is the second member and is not yet moved; Privacy and Credits stay places.

## Measured

Desktop and phone, Explore in the Italian after 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6, with a probe on `main[data-shell]`:
URL, probe, occupied squares and title identical before and after every way of closing (Escape, Close,
scrim, Back, Forward-then-Escape, keyboard). The board behind repaints on a theme pick with the layer open
(`d4` `rgb(76,76,84)` → `rgb(59,80,104)`). Focus returns to the gear; the ring shows only when it was
opened from the keyboard. The page never scrolls; a scrolled list keeps its 400px through open and close.
320px bar: `scrollWidth 320`.

## Afternoon: the report joins

The owner answered: move `/report`, keep new-tab links, leave the drawer alone. The report is a form, so
the questions were what happens to half-typed words (kept in `sessionStorage`, restored on open, cleared
on send) and to the server's answer (kept in the layer — `use:enhance`'s default would have re-run the
drill's `load` on success and swapped the drill for the 500 page on a thrown action, which was measured
before it was fixed). Sent for real against the local D1; the phone reaches it through Settings in the
same dialog.

## Found on the way

- Closing the layer restarted the drill: SvelteKit assigns a new `URL` object to `page.url` on every
  history step, and the session-start effect read it. It reads two derived values now.
- `opener.focus()` scrolls a long page to the bar. `preventScroll: true`.
- A shallow entry remembers the page *beneath* its URL, so Back from a link out of the layer returns to
  that page with the layer open. A guard written for the opposite was removed.
- In the CDP harness, a mouse event under touch emulation never returns; phone runs use touch points.
