## Line map readable on phones
- Owner on a phone: map lines invisible, titles unreadable. See [[2026-10-06 On a phone the map's ink and titles are sized for the screen]].
- `LineMap.svelte`: zoom-compensated ink, halo on found lines, theme-derived undiscovered colour. `linemap.ts`: touch title row, narrow gutter.
- Staging badge moved to the bottom-left; it covered the map title.
- Verified with headless Chromium at 390×844 (abyss, paper, ember themes) on staging. Not yet on production.
- Headless screenshots on NixOS: Playwright's bundled Chromium lacks libs; use `nix shell nixpkgs#chromium` and pass `executablePath`.

## Map trees hang from their titles
- Owner: a tree's first branch sat nearer the next band's title than its own. Centred tidy placement put the trunk mid-band.
- Touch layout now top-aligns (`place(..., 'top')`): every parent sits level with its first child, so the trunk runs right under the title and branches drop below. Extra gap before the next band. New unit test.
- Trade-off: a band with many sidelines (Caro-Kann "Sidelines") becomes a comb of steep drops from the trunk. Readable, but a candidate for elbow edges if it looks busy.
